# Counting Sheep of SPLORR!!

An incremental about counting a million card sheep to fall asleep, for Ultimate AI-Powered Game Jam #7 (themes DREAM and STAGE): send sheep over the fence into a Connect Four fold, score them like cribbage, sink deeper through the stages of sleep, buy sheepdogs. Made by an AI agent (Claude Code) at the direction of TheGrumpyGameDev. See `DESIGN.md`; page copy in `ITCH_DESCRIPTION.md`.

Play: no public build yet (GitHub Pages is the user's call). Locally: `tools/serve.sh`, then http://localhost:8000/.
