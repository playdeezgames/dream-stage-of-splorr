## Page text

**Latest version:** https://playdeezgames.github.io/dream-stage-of-splorr/ (GitHub Pages since November 13, 2026; also add it as an external link on the page)

**Counting Sheep of SPLORR!!** is an incremental about the oldest trick for falling asleep.

The insomniac can't sleep. The pasture is full of sheep, a million of them, and every one is a playing card: its rank on its side, its suit in the colour of its wool, and a black face if it's a face card. Pick them up, carry them to the fence and send them over one at a time. Every sheep that jumps is counted.

Over the fence is the sheepfold: seven pens, six high, and a sheep drops to the bottom of its pen like a counter in Connect Four. **Four of a suit in a row** (across, down or diagonal) fall asleep and float away, and they score as a **cribbage hand**, with the moon as the cut card: fifteens, pairs, runs, the flush and his nobs. Sheep above drop into the gaps, so lines can chain. Every jump is pegged too, the way cribbage is played: fifteen for two, thirty-one for two, pairs, runs and the go.

Points move a peg along a 121-hole cribbage board, and every lap takes the insomniac a stage deeper into sleep: drowsy, then N1, N2, N3 and REM, cycle after cycle, with a new moon each time and more Zzz for every point. Spend yer Zzz at the nightstand on a bigger armful, greener grass (more sheep), counting by twos, fives and tens and on up, sheepdogs that fetch and send sheep for you, and faster dogs. Don't dawdle: a sheep left in the fold too long dozes off and drifts away unscored.

Count the millionth sheep and the insomniac is finally out like a light. A run takes about two hours; yer best nights are kept.

### The themes

- **DREAM:** the whole game is the trip from wide awake to asleep. Counting sheep is the dream's door, the moon cuts the cards, sheep doze off, and dream logic lets you count by a thousand.
- **STAGE:** the stages of sleep. Each lap of the cribbage board is a stage deeper (N1, N2, N3, REM, then round again), and each stage brings a new moon and a bigger reward. And the sheep do jump up onto a stage of sorts: the fold.

### How to play

- **Keyboard:** arrow keys or WASD to walk, Space for OK, Esc to cancel. Walk into a sheep to pick it up. Stand at the fence under a pen and press Space (or walk into the fence) to send a sheep over. Walk into the nightstand in the bottom-right corner to shop: Up and Down choose, Space buys, Esc leaves; on "Clear a pen", Left and Right choose the pen. Esc out in the pasture opens a menu: carry on, sound on or off, start over. F sends feedback.
- **Gamepad:** d-pad or left stick to walk, A for OK, B to cancel.
- **Touch:** the pads beside the screen walk, OK and Back.

The night is saved in the browser as you play. A fresh night (Esc, Start over, or the link under the screen) asks first, and keeps yer bests.
